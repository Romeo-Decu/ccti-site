Synergetic Solutions | Beyond Advice

At CCTI we do not treat innovation as a theoretical exercise, and we do not produce reports for their own sake. Synergetic Solutions is how we help organizations turn new concepts into viable commercial and technological results.

We do not rewrite your business. We build on the foundation you already have (your roots) and step in where conventional advice stops (beyond advice): with applied research, modelling, and the ability to develop and validate in our own laboratory.

Strategy, practiced

Our approach is metastrategy: understanding reality as it is, not as we would like it to be — beyond perceptions and points of view — and analysing the consequences of every action and every inaction before acting, in order to achieve your interests and shape both the present and the future.

In practice, this means one thing: deliberately directing actions and inactions to control their consequences and achieve your interests.

Our strategic consulting draws on more than twenty years of commercial leadership, business development and organizational transformation across international, multi-industry companies, and on applied research in our own laboratory.

We do not arrive with ready-made answers and we do not deliver paper. You know your organization better than anyone; we bring method, evidence and an outside view, and we work alongside your team until the result can be verified.

Validation & execution matrix

The services are decoupled and modular: you enter at the stage that matches your project's technological maturity (TRL), for one stage or for the whole path. Each stage ends with a verifiable deliverable and a decision: continue, adjust or stop.

[01] · TRL 1–2

Opportunity & Concept Development

  • Structural audit of the technological opportunity
  • Rigorous problem formulation: variables, constraints, measurable success criteria
  • Preliminary modelling of the concept (digital or computational model)
  • Deliverable: Concept brief: the problem stated precisely, the hypotheses to test and the architectural risks identified before major resources are committed.
[02] · TRL 2–4

Innovation & Business Strategy

  • Business-model architecture for deep-tech
  • Positioning of the emerging technology: markets, competitors, alternatives
  • Mapping of international scaling paths
  • Deliverable: Commercial map of the innovation: who pays, for what, and how it reaches sustainable revenue.
[03] · TRL 3–5

Feasibility & Scientific Validation

  • Physical and computational analyses (thermodynamics, informatics)
  • Technical feasibility audit and algorithmic due diligence
  • Empirical and experimental validation of the working hypotheses
  • Deliverable: Validation report with data, methods and stated limits, usable in investor and funder due diligence.
[04] · TRL 4–7

Development & Implementation

  • Technical support for converging hardware-software prototyping
  • Integration of AI agents and on-board computing (edge computing)
  • Management of test protocols in complex environments
  • Deliverable: From blueprint to a working prototype, tested in relevant conditions.
[05] · TRL 6–9

Market Development

  • Cross-border go-to-market strategy
  • Identification and mapping of technology distribution networks
  • Structuring international consortia and funding opportunities
  • Deliverable: Initial commercial traction: first customers and a measurable test of demand.
